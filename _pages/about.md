I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. I am currently a Research Intern at MINIMAX (February 2025 - Present).

My research interests include:
* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
